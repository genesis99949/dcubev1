# Stickere One-Page Express

Generate cu instrumentul integrat imagegen, după referința de stickere japoneze oferită de Ramon. PNG-urile originale și variantele WebP de 640px cu transparența păstrată sunt în `public/images/express/stickers/`. Nu sunt încă montate în hero.

## Prompt comun

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE standalone die-cut sticker for the Romanian One-Page Express website, inspired by the attached reference of exuberant vintage Japanese shop stickers. Original artwork, not copying characters. Flat bold screenprint illustration, quirky hand-inked thick black outlines, chunky playful silhouettes, restrained tiny print imperfections, crisp edges, white die-cut border. Palette STRICTLY crimson #D7141E, lemon yellow #F6E21C, black #111110 and warm white #F2F1EC. No orange, blue or green. Genuine transparent alpha background outside the sticker, no paper sheet, no checkerboard, no backdrop, no cast shadow. Single centered sticker, complete silhouette with ample transparent margin, high resolution, readable at 150px.

## Subiecte adăugate la promptul comun

### pisica-laptop

A cheerful lucky beckoning cat (maneki-neko), warm-white fur with red patches, black details and yellow bell, leaning over a small open black laptop with a red screen and one yellow sparkle. Welcoming, expressive, handmade Japanese advertising mascot energy. No lettering, no Japanese characters, no money or coins.

### racheta-express

A stubby red delivery rocket with yellow flame, white window with black outline, flying diagonally upward with 2 short chunky speed streaks. Playful vintage Japanese advertising sticker silhouette, compact and dynamic. No text.

### browser-zambitor

A friendly anthropomorphic small website browser window: warm-white rounded rectangular body with thick black outline, red top bar, two black eyes and a cheerful smile, little yellow gloved arms and red shoes, holding a small yellow star. A cohesive retro cartoon shop mascot sticker, not glossy, no text.

### cursor-fulger

A bold oversized warm-white computer pointer arrow with thick black outline, grabbing a crimson lightning bolt, lemon-yellow comic speed accents. Punchy compact diagonally tilted die-cut graphic, vintage Japanese pop sticker visual language, not a conventional flat app icon. No text.

### 48h-express

A bold retro Japanese discount-label-inspired die-cut sticker, irregular lemon yellow starburst with thick black keyline and crimson offset ink outline, a small red swoosh and only this exact text: '48h' in very chunky black hand-lettering. Small white accent highlights. The whole silhouette is the sticker; outside is transparent. No other text, no prices, no Japanese lettering.
